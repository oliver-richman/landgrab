# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release.

- A game mode that confines you to a single chunk, with land bought outward one chunk at
  a time. Enabled by a tick box on the Game tab when creating a world.
- Locked chunks are hidden behind an electric haze. Touching the boundary hurts and throws
  you back.
- A phone in the inventory with three apps: a map for buying land, a counter for selling
  goods, and a message list.
- Prices derived from the world's own recipes, so every item is worth something and no
  amount of crafting turns a profit.
- Advancements pay out, graded by the game's own difficulty rating.
- The Nether and the End hold their own territory, claimed on first arrival. Portals into
  land you do not own will not fire.
